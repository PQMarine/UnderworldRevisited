-- help, modern
# The pointer / The mouse either turns the view (pointer locked, a crosshair in the middle) or moves a free pointer over the windows and the world. Only the right button switches between the two.
Right Button | Free the pointer, or lock it again to look around. The game menu has the other way round as an option: the pointer stays free and the view turns while the button is held. |
Locked | The crosshair aims: "e", "q" and the left button act on what it is on. |
Free | The windows take the clicks; "e" and "q" act on what is under the pointer, in the world as in the bags, the character panel and the action bar (holding "e" does nothing over a window). |
# Moving / -
W A S D | Walk forward and back, step sideways. |
Left Shift | Held: walk slowly instead of running. The Controls dialog can make it switch on and off. |
Left Alt + W A S D / X | Easy movement: one step forward or back, or a turn of 45 degrees; held, it repeats. X steps forward without stepping off a ledge. |
Mouse | Turn and look up and down (pointer locked). |
Space | Jump. |
Space / Left Control | While flying or levitating: rise and sink. |
# Acting / -
"e" (tap) | The usual thing for what you aim at: pick it up, talk, open a door, pull a lever, use it. |
"e" (hold) | Use it directly, even what could be picked up - a sack is emptied instead of taken. |
"q" | Look at it. |
Left Button (free pointer) | On a thing in the world: its menu - Talk, Use, Look, Pick up. Held and dragged: take it onto the pointer. |
# Combat / -
"r" | Draw or put away the weapon in your hand. |
Left Button (held) | Charge the weapon; let go to strike. The power gem shows the charge. |
The strike | Pointer locked: look up for an overhead bash, down for a thrust, straight ahead for a slash. Pointer free: the upper third of the screen bashes, the lower third thrusts, the middle slashes. |
Bow, sling | Charge as above; the red target circle shows when the charge is enough. The shot flies to the free pointer, else to the crosshair. |
# Magic / -
Z | The rune panel. Its handle at the left edge opens it as well, and its pin keeps it out. |
Runes tab | Click a rune to lay it in the hollow (the fourth pushes out the first), right click to look at it, the strip below the hollow clears it. Click the hollow to cast, or drag it onto the action bar. |
Spells tab | Every spell by circle, grey what you cannot cast now. Click one to lay its runes, drag it onto the action bar. |
Targets | A spell that needs a target flies at once at the crosshair with the pointer locked. With the pointer free the target cursor waits for a left click; Escape lets the spell go. |
Active spells | Top right. Left click: what it does; right click: end it. |
# The bags and the character / -
"b" | Open or close the bags. The backpack always shows at the bottom right. |
"c" | The character panel: the paperdoll, your attributes and skills. Its pin keeps it out. |
Left Button | Take a thing and put it down - drag it, or click and click. On a container it goes inside; outside the windows it is dropped (low on the screen) or thrown (higher). |
Shift + Left Button | Split a stack. |
Right Button | A thing's menu: Use, Equip, Look, Pick up, Split. A container opens or closes at once (Shift for its menu). What is used on something else - a key, the oil flask - then waits for a left click on its target. |
The head | Food and drink put down on the paperdoll's head are eaten. |
1 ... 0 | The action bar. Drop a thing or drag a spell onto a slot; the key or a click uses it, a weapon goes into your hand. Drag a slot off the bar to empty it. |
Sleep, Track | The bedroll and the boots beside the paperdoll's feet - or F10 and F9. |
# Conversation / -
1 ... 9 | Choose an answer - or click it. The mouse wheel scrolls the conversation. |
Trading | Drag your things from the bags onto "Your offer". A left click on a trade slot marks it for the deal (a gold ring), a right click looks at it; drag your offer back out when you change your mind. |
# Map, menu and the rest / -
"m" | The big map, with the map in your pack. The minimap top right: + and - or the mouse wheel zoom it, a click opens the big map. |
Tab | This help, in the character panel. |
Escape | Closes what is open - a waiting spell, the bags, the panels - then the game menu (F1 as well). |
Right Ctrl + S / R | Save, restore. |
Shift + F2 | Switch to the classic controls. |

Left Button | In the view: walk towards the pointer, held. On the frame: the icons, the runes, the compass, and taking and putting down things. |

Shift + F2 | Switch to the modern controls. |

# The pointer / The mouse either turns the view (pointer locked, a crosshair in the middle) or moves a free pointer over the windows and the world. Only the right button switches between the two.
Right Button | Free the pointer, or lock it again to look around. The game menu has the other way round as an option: the pointer stays free and the view turns while the button is held. |
Locked | The crosshair aims: "e", "q" and the left button act on what it is on. |
Free | The windows take the clicks; "e" and "q" act on what is under the pointer, in the world as in the bags, the character panel and the action bar (holding "e" does nothing over a window). |
# Moving / -
T A S D | Walk forward and back, step sideways. |
Left Shift | Held: walk slowly instead of running. The Controls dialog can make it switch on and off. |
Left Alt + T A S D / X | Easy movement: one step forward or back, or a turn of 45 degrees; held, it repeats. X steps forward without stepping off a ledge. |
Mouse | Turn and look up and down (pointer locked). |
Space | Jump. |
Space / Left Control | While flying or levitating: rise and sink. |
# Acting / -
"e" (tap) | The usual thing for what you aim at: pick it up, talk, open a door, pull a lever, use it. |
"e" (hold) | Use it directly, even what could be picked up - a sack is emptied instead of taken. |
"q" | Look at it. |
Left Button (free pointer) | On a thing in the world: its menu - Talk, Use, Look, Pick up. Held and dragged: take it onto the pointer. |
# Combat / -
"r" | Draw or put away the weapon in your hand. |
Left Button (held) | Charge the weapon; let go to strike. The power gem shows the charge. |
The strike | Pointer locked: look up for an overhead bash, down for a thrust, straight ahead for a slash. Pointer free: the upper third of the screen bashes, the lower third thrusts, the middle slashes. |
Bow, sling | Charge as above; the red target circle shows when the charge is enough. The shot flies to the free pointer, else to the crosshair. |
# Magic / -
Z | The rune panel. Its handle at the left edge opens it as well, and its pin keeps it out. |
Runes tab | Click a rune to lay it in the hollow (the fourth pushes out the first), right click to look at it, the strip below the hollow clears it. Click the hollow to cast, or drag it onto the action bar. |
Spells tab | Every spell by circle, grey what you cannot cast now. Click one to lay its runes, drag it onto the action bar. |
Targets | A spell that needs a target flies at once at the crosshair with the pointer locked. With the pointer free the target cursor waits for a left click; Escape lets the spell go. |
Active spells | Top right. Left click: what it does; right click: end it. |
# The bags and the character / -
"b" | Open or close the bags. The backpack always shows at the bottom right. |
"c" | The character panel: the paperdoll, your attributes and skills. Its pin keeps it out. |
Left Button | Take a thing and put it down - drag it, or click and click. On a container it goes inside; outside the windows it is dropped (low on the screen) or thrown (higher). |
Shift + Left Button | Split a stack. |
Right Button | A thing's menu: Use, Equip, Look, Pick up, Split. A container opens or closes at once (Shift for its menu). What is used on something else - a key, the oil flask - then waits for a left click on its target. |
The head | Food and drink put down on the paperdoll's head are eaten. |
1 ... 0 | The action bar. Drop a thing or drag a spell onto a slot; the key or a click uses it, a weapon goes into your hand. Drag a slot off the bar to empty it. |
Sleep, Track | The bedroll and the boots beside the paperdoll's feet - or F10 and F9. |
# Conversation / -
1 ... 9 | Choose an answer - or click it. The mouse wheel scrolls the conversation. |
Trading | Drag your things from the bags onto "Your offer". A left click on a trade slot marks it for the deal (a gold ring), a right click looks at it; drag your offer back out when you change your mind. |
# Map, menu and the rest / -
"m" | The big map, with the map in your pack. The minimap top right: + and - or the mouse wheel zoom it, a click opens the big map. |
Tab | This help, in the character panel. |
Escape | Closes what is open - a waiting spell, the bags, the panels - then the game menu (F1 as well). |
Right Ctrl + S / R | Save, restore. |
Shift + F2 | Switch to the classic controls. |

# The pointer / The mouse either turns the view (pointer locked, a crosshair in the middle) or moves a free pointer over the windows and the world. Only the right button switches between the two.
Middle Button | Free the pointer, or lock it again to look around. The game menu has the other way round as an option: the pointer stays free and the view turns while the button is held. |
Locked | The crosshair aims: F, "q" and the left button act on what it is on. |
Free | The windows take the clicks; F and "q" act on what is under the pointer, in the world as in the bags, the character panel and the action bar (holding F does nothing over a window). |
# Moving / -
Up Arrow Left Arrow Down Arrow Right Arrow | Walk forward and back, step sideways. |
Caps Lock | Held: walk slowly instead of running. The Controls dialog can make it switch on and off. |
Right Alt + Up Arrow Left Arrow Down Arrow Right Arrow / X | Easy movement: one step forward or back, or a turn of 45 degrees; held, it repeats. X steps forward without stepping off a ledge. |
Mouse | Turn and look up and down (pointer locked). |
Space | Jump. |
Space / Left Control | While flying or levitating: rise and sink. |
# Acting / -
F (tap) | The usual thing for what you aim at: pick it up, talk, open a door, pull a lever, use it. |
F (hold) | Use it directly, even what could be picked up - a sack is emptied instead of taken. |
"q" | Look at it. |
G (free pointer) | On a thing in the world: its menu - Talk, Use, Look, Pick up. Held and dragged: take it onto the pointer. |
# Combat / -
"r" | Draw or put away the weapon in your hand. |
G (held) | Charge the weapon; let go to strike. The power gem shows the charge. |
The strike | Pointer locked: look up for an overhead bash, down for a thrust, straight ahead for a slash. Pointer free: the upper third of the screen bashes, the lower third thrusts, the middle slashes. |
Bow, sling | Charge as above; the red target circle shows when the charge is enough. The shot flies to the free pointer, else to the crosshair. |
# Magic / -
Z | The rune panel. Its handle at the left edge opens it as well, and its pin keeps it out. |
Runes tab | Click a rune to lay it in the hollow (the fourth pushes out the first), right click to look at it, the strip below the hollow clears it. Click the hollow to cast, or drag it onto the action bar. |
Spells tab | Every spell by circle, grey what you cannot cast now. Click one to lay its runes, drag it onto the action bar. |
Targets | A spell that needs a target flies at once at the crosshair with the pointer locked. With the pointer free the target cursor waits for a left click; Escape lets the spell go. |
Active spells | Top right. Left click: what it does; right click: end it. |
# The bags and the character / -
"b" | Open or close the bags. The backpack always shows at the bottom right. |
"c" | The character panel: the paperdoll, your attributes and skills. Its pin keeps it out. |
G | Take a thing and put it down - drag it, or click and click. On a container it goes inside; outside the windows it is dropped (low on the screen) or thrown (higher). |
Shift + G | Split a stack. |
Middle Button | A thing's menu: Use, Equip, Look, Pick up, Split. A container opens or closes at once (Shift for its menu). What is used on something else - a key, the oil flask - then waits for a left click on its target. |
The head | Food and drink put down on the paperdoll's head are eaten. |
1 ... 0 | The action bar. Drop a thing or drag a spell onto a slot; the key or a click uses it, a weapon goes into your hand. Drag a slot off the bar to empty it. |
Sleep, Track | The bedroll and the boots beside the paperdoll's feet - or F10 and F9. |
# Conversation / -
1 ... 9 | Choose an answer - or click it. The mouse wheel scrolls the conversation. |
Trading | Drag your things from the bags onto "Your offer". A left click on a trade slot marks it for the deal (a gold ring), a right click looks at it; drag your offer back out when you change your mind. |
# Map, menu and the rest / -
Numpad 5 | The big map, with the map in your pack. The minimap top right: + and - or the mouse wheel zoom it, a click opens the big map. |
Tab | This help, in the character panel. |
Escape | Closes what is open - a waiting spell, the bags, the panels - then the game menu (F1 as well). |
Right Ctrl + S / R | Save, restore. |
Shift + F2 | Switch to the classic controls. |
-- help, classic
